### 1. 参与范围

本 mod（《巨型水箱 / Mega Water Tank》）的**大部分代码、文档与美术资源**是在
**生成式 AI 辅助**下完成的，具体包括：

- C# 源码：结构设计、补丁实现、注释与重构建议

**人工部分**：需求定义、游戏机制核对（反编译阅读与实机验证）、补丁注入点选择、

## English

### 1. Scope of AI involvement

Most of the **code, documentation and artwork** in this mod
("Mega Water Tank") was produced with **generative AI assistance**, including:

- C# sources: structure, patch implementations, comments, refactoring suggestions
- Item sprites (`Textures/Items/*.png`): generated by scripts with AI assistance, then hand-tuned
- Documentation (README, changelog, developer notes, test sheets)
- Localization (Chinese / English / Russian)

**Human contributions**: requirements, game-mechanics verification (reading decompiled
assemblies and in-game testing), choice of patch injection points, all in-game acceptance
tests (filling, overflow, refill, save/load, compatibility matrix with other mods,
performance and log review), and release decisions.
